1. The family lounge winter garden

This direction is ideal when the space needs to work as a real everyday room. The roof choice matters because it controls warmth, glare and how enclosed the room feels in colder months. The side choice matters because it changes how naturally the home opens into the garden in spring and summer.

Best roof direction:Fixed glass or a carefully balanced roof mix when natural light is a major priority.
Best side direction:Sliding doors or wide bifold openings with fixed glazed returns.

2. The dining & hosting extension

When the space is used to entertain, circulation becomes everything. You want the opening back to the house, the terrace transition and the furniture planning to feel effortless. This is where custom fabrication makes a visible difference.

Best roof direction:Fixed glass for brightness or bioclimatic solutions for flexibility.
Best side direction:Bifold or sliding frontages with clean sightlines.

3. The private garden room / office

This brief needs comfort and focus. It usually benefits from more controlled glazing, selected privacy zones and better temperature stability throughout the year.

Best roof direction:Thermally conscious fixed glass, insulated combinations or solid polycarbonate depending on light preference.
Best side direction:More fixed glass, carefully placed openings and optional solid sections.

4. The leisure / spa-style enclosure

Some clients want the room to feel calm, premium and hotel-like. In these projects, subtle material choices, privacy and lighting readiness are just as important as the structural system itself.

Best roof direction:Premium fixed glass or a refined bioclimatic route depending on how flexible the roof needs to be.
Best side direction:Fixed glass, selected sliding elements and more private boundary treatment.
Why Hawk works well for homes

Because the finished feeling matters.

Homeowners do not just buy a roof and a few glazed sides. They buy the feeling of the space once the work is complete. That feeling comes from proportion, light, opening width, transitions, warmth and finish quality.

Better material direction

We can design around premium aluminium sections and double-glazed strategies that aim for a sharper, more dependable result.

Better visual balance

The right project often mixes transparency and solidity. Fully custom design helps avoid the awkward “too much glass, too little comfort” problem.

Better long-term value

When the structure feels designed with the house rather than attached to it, the space tends to look more expensive and more intentional.
