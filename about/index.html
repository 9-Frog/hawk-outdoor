About Hawk Outdoor

A Turkish-owned, design-led outdoor living business built around bespoke work.

Hawk Construction / Hawk Outdoor is a Turkish-owned and operated business with more than 15 years of combined experience in pergolas, winter gardens and outdoor living systems. Our aim is simple: help people create better spaces in which they experience life — spaces that look beautiful, feel comfortable and remain useful through more of the year.

Our mission

To help clients build their own comfort zone.

At Hawk Outdoor, our mission is to make good design, quality products and trustworthy guidance more accessible. We believe outdoor space should not be wasted or treated like a luxury that only matters for a few sunny weeks each year. With the right system, the right detailing and the right advice, outdoor living can become a real part of everyday life.

Customised to suit you

Our surveyors work closely with you to review the space you already have, understand how you want to use it and turn that brief into a structure that actually fits the property.

Support through the process

Our team works with clients through budget planning, design development, practical guidance and the statutory considerations that often come with larger outdoor structures.

Still built around people

Even as the business grows, the core idea remains the same: help families and businesses love life outdoors with better design and a finish they can be proud of.

What makes our service different

  • We focus on bespoke solutions instead of pushing every project into a standard module.
  • We care about year-round usability, not just the first visual impression.
  • We help clients choose the right combination of roof, sides, openings and materials.
  • We aim to deliver premium-looking systems at value-conscious prices.

What we build

  • Winter gardens and glazed outdoor rooms
  • Pergolas and bioclimatic pergolas
  • Restaurant, café and commercial terrace covers
  • Sliding doors, bifolds, fixed glass, guillotine systems and insulated panel sections
