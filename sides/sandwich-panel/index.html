Sandwich Panel

Sandwich Panel

Sandwich panels are useful wherever privacy, durability, insulation or service practicality matter more than fully transparent glazing. They are often under-used in design conversations, but in the right place they can dramatically improve performance and make the whole project more balanced.

Stronger practicality

Better design balance

A project does not have to be fully glazed to look premium. Well-placed solid sections can improve the result.
